Soft delete lets you recover blobs deleted or overwritten by mistake or by ransomware.

**Frameworks** (indicative)

- Themes: Recovery of deleted or overwritten data
- MCSB: BR-1
- CIS Controls v8: 11.1
- NIST CSF 2.0: PR.DS, RC.RP
- NIST 800-53: CP-9
- CMMC / 800-171: MP.L2-3.8.9
- SOC 2: A1.2
- ISO 27001: 8.13

**Remediation Action**

1. Storage account → Data protection → Enable soft delete for blobs, 7 days or more.

**Links**
- [Soft delete for blobs](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/storage/blobs/soft-delete-blob-overview)
